About

A couple of years ago I started collecting orienteering maps - the ones from races I ran, the HITTA and Mapico permanent courses I stumbled upon, and a few that friends handed me after a training. Before I knew it I had a drawer full of PDFs, phone photos and crumpled paper, and no idea anymore which map was where. So I built myself a little tool to keep track of them all. Every map gets its versions, its events and its courses, and - this is the part I'm really excited about - I place each map on top of the aerial photos, so you can see exactly where it lies in the real world. Slide the opacity back and forth on a map page and you'll see what I mean: it's a wonderful rabbithole. This site is the public part of that collection. Search for a place, a club or an event, zoom in on the map, and have a look around. Maybe you'll find a forest you ran years ago, or a park you never knew had a map at all. Hope you enjoy it as much as I do. And if you spot a mistake, or have a map I really should add: let me know! Cheers, Rik
